Silver Crash: Technical Washout Meets Fundamental Headwinds

Silver is experiencing relentless selling pressure, trading at its widest discount to its 200-day moving average since the Covid crisis liquidation. Speculative bullish positioning has been thoroughly wiped out, bringing pivotal technical support levels into focus. Crucially, this rout extends beyond a simple positioning washout. A strengthening US dollar and softening industrial demand continue to weigh heavily on physical demand. While the technical dislocation is severe, extreme oversold readings alone cannot trigger a durable rebound without stabilizing fundamental trends.

01

Silver trades 25% below 200-day average in historic washout

Silver has plunged roughly 25% below its 200-day moving average, marking the widest technical discount since the March 2020 liquidity crunch. Prior to the pandemic, a dislocation of this magnitude had not occurred since 2013.

This drop represents one of the most severe technical washouts in the metal's history. Investors now face a critical test: determining whether this move signals final exhaustion selling or a broader structural repricing driven by weakening industrial demand.

02

Silver reaches extreme multi-year oversold territory

Silver is testing some of its most extreme technical oversold readings in years. Momentum indicators have dropped back to the lower boundary near 25—a zone that previously aligned with local rebounds in spring 2025 and summer 2026.

However, extreme oversold readings alone do not guarantee an immediate bounce. During strong downward trends, markets can remain oversold far longer than most traders anticipate, requiring clear signs of price stabilization before a durable floor forms.

03

Speculative silver bullish bets fall to lowest level since early 2024

The positioning washout in silver continues to deepen. According to JPMorgan, managed money net long exposure has dropped to its lowest level since the first quarter of 2024.

With prices trading roughly 25% below the 200-day moving average, speculative positioning appears thoroughly cleared out. Still, a positioning reset and a durable fundamental bottom are two distinct things. A sustainable recovery will require improving macro and industrial catalysts beyond technical exhaustion.

04

Slumping solar demand creates a fundamental headwind for silver

The silver selloff goes beyond a simple positioning unwind. Demand from the solar industry, a crucial structural growth engine for the metal, is projected to drop by roughly 30% this year (around 60 million ounces), with weakness expected to persist into 2027.

According to JPMorgan, this marks a major shift for a market long supported by structural supply deficits. The core risk for investors is that physical silver balances could tip into surplus right as speculative positioning finishes washing out.

05

US dollar strength remains the primary headwind for silver

A stronger US dollar continues to weigh directly on silver prices. This straightforward inverse relationship has dictated the metal's trajectory throughout the entire year.

Comparing silver against the inverted US Dollar Index (DXY) shows the two assets moving in tight lockstep. As long as the greenback maintains its upward momentum, sustained relief rallies in silver will likely face persistent macro resistance
